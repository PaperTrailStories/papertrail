Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026. This policy covers the Paper Trail integration with TikTok's Content Posting API.

1. What we handle

The integration handles only what is needed to publish videos to the account owner's own TikTok profile: the video files, captions, and posting preferences the owner provides.

2. What we don't collect

No personal data is collected from viewers. No analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising identifiers are used by these pages.

3. Sharing

Content is transmitted to TikTok solely to complete the owner's publishing requests. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

4. Retention

Uploaded materials are kept only as long as needed to operate the integration.
